# ClosetMac — App Store Listing

## Tagline
**"Your wardrobe, curated."**

---

## Description

ClosetMac brings the warmth of a personal stylist to your Mac. Track every piece you own, discover what to wear based on the weather and your mood, and finally build a wardrobe that feels like *you*.

### Features

- **Visual Closet** — Browse your entire wardrobe at a glance with rich previews
- **Weather-Aware Styling** — Get outfit suggestions tuned to today's temperature and conditions
- **Mood-Based Looks** — Set your vibe and discover combinations that match how you feel
- **Outfit Builder** — Mix and match tops, bottoms, shoes, and accessories into cohesive outfits
- **Wear Logging** — Track what's getting love and what's gathering dust
- **Wishlist** — Keep a curated list of what's missing from your wardrobe
- **Style Insights** — See your wardrobe's color balance and most-worn staples

### Aesthetic

Warm boutique. Earthy tones. ClosetMac feels like a well-organized closet in a sunlit apartment — not a department store.
